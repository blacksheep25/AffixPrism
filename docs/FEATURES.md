
### Local dashboard usability update

- Shortlist displays market favourites and can refresh their saved prices. Older favourites without a category can be re-saved from Market Prices to enable refresh.
- Session uses separate loot and area-history panels. Rune Helper has its own page; Encounters loads the selected reward category.
- Item inspection uses the Alt+E evaluation window, including the tray clipboard action. The redundant navigation entry is hidden.
- Home supports a local character name, class, level and portrait through Edit character. This is not an automatic account import.
- Hover a market price for fresh Divine, Exalted and Chaos exchange equivalents. These are conversions, not evidence of listings in each currency. Hover history for provider samples, relative range and change details; the source does not provide individual sale prices or exact sample timestamps.
- Trade Research explains its workflow and compares resale candidates in one converted currency. Corruption premiums require paired variants from the provider; missing pairs are explained rather than estimated without evidence.
